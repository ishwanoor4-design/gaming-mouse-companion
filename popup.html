Mouse Troubleshooting

Mouse feels too fast?

Try lowering your in-game sensitivity first. Change one setting at a time and test it in-game.

Mouse feels too slow?

Try increasing sensitivity gradually, or test a different DPI setting that suits your playing style.

Mouse movement feels inconsistent?

Check your mouse surface, battery or cable, sensor cleanliness, game settings, and any active acceleration.
